# 0001: Couples Parity and Tiered Leaderboards with Non-Spendable Karma

We chose to decouple competitive stakes from point expenditure by establishing 3 reset cycles (Weekly, Monthly, Yearly) alongside a permanent, non-spendable Karma score. Point values are calibrated symmetrically between Machek and Myra's distinct habits (e.g. Swedish vs Polish, Basketball vs Running) to guarantee equal earning potential without forcing identical lifestyles.

### Context & Trade-off
Treating points as a spendable currency would penalize players for redeeming earned stakes (e.g. buying dinner) and distort leaderboard rankings. By separating time-windowed competitive scores from lifetime Karma, players retain bragging rights while still competing for active stakes.
