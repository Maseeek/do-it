# Character progression and TikTok direction

This extends the marketing research in [issue #46](https://github.com/Maseeek/do-it/issues/46) with Maciek's request for an upgradeable character and TikTok marketing. It follows the confirmed character direction in [#33](https://github.com/Maseeek/do-it/issues/33) and its workstreams. It is a marketing and product proposal, not a character implementation or an approved asset specification.

## The story to sell

Two people build different habits. Their characters gradually show the progress they have earned. They compete for the week, see the outcome together, and have a reason for a rematch.

That gives short videos something visible to reveal: a starter look, a real habit behind an earned upgrade, a rival's reaction, and a weekly result. This is a creative hypothesis. We have not measured whether characters improve acquisition or retention.

Keep the emotional headline from [the positioning brief](positioning.md):

> A little rivalry. A better us.

For the eventual character release, test this supporting copy:

> Choose your own habits. Earn new looks for your character. Challenge your favourite person for the week.

Then demonstrate the separate plans, matched available weekly points, and the agreed Stake. The headline does not have to explain every mechanic.

An alternative focused on identity and pride:

> Build a character you're proud to show off.

Supporting line:

> Earn your look through the habits you keep. Bring it into a duel with your person.

These are proposed lines for a planned feature. The current implementation has Karma-based Player color unlocks, not the full outfit and character system. Pre-release clips should be clearly described as work in progress.

## Make upgrading feel earned

The confirmed direction already distinguishes permanent progress from the weekly contest:

1. Every Player starts with a basic avatar immediately.
2. Habit Check-ins add lifetime Karma. Show the next available cosmetic and progress toward it.
3. Reaching an approved milestone unlocks a color, suit, hat, or accessory. The Player chooses what to equip.
4. The Duel displays both Players' chosen characters, with body color matching the app accent.
5. The weekly Leaderboard Score starts a new contest while lifetime Karma and earned cosmetic access remain.

Specific achievements may unlock additional cosmetics, subject to the rules in [#35](https://github.com/Maseeek/do-it/issues/35). Exact milestones and items are still design decisions. Preserve the existing earned colors when introducing characters.

Keep the first release focused on Profile customization and the Duel. The design lead determines the original character art, asset format, and accessory combinations in [#34](https://github.com/Maseeek/do-it/issues/34). The later two-character result scenes in [#38](https://github.com/Maseeek/do-it/issues/38) depict the already-recorded Stake outcome. They do not decide who wins.

## Competitor overlap

[Habitica](https://www.habitica.com/) already connects real-world task completion to Avatar levels, equipment, and game progression. [Finch's official outfit guide](https://help.finchcare.com/hc/en-us/articles/37935977276813-Shops-in-Finch-Outfits-Travel-and-More) describes clothing and accessories bought with Rainbow Stones, and [its adventure guide](https://help.finchcare.com/hc/en-us/articles/37779979512845-Going-on-an-Adventure) connects adventures with the birb's growth.

An upgradeable character is therefore a familiar attraction, not a verified market gap. Do It's more specific framing combines two Player identities, separate personal habits, matched weekly point potential, and a Stake the pair agrees on. Showing a real pair's recurring rivalry may make that combination easier to understand and follow. Neither competitor page nor this proposal establishes exclusive mechanics or TikTok performance.

## A first short video

Proposed 15-20 second concept, once the relevant feature is available. The time ranges are an editorial proposal, not a platform requirement.

| Beat | Shot | Spoken or on-screen line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3 seconds | Both real Players, then their starter avatars. | "We made our habits a competition." |
| 3-8 seconds | One Player runs; the other reads. Show the two plans' matched weekly points. | "Different habits. Same weekly points available." |
| 8-13 seconds | Show an actual earned cosmetic unlock and equip it. Cut to the other Player's reaction. | "I kept showing up. My character got an upgrade." |
| 13-20 seconds | Show the recorded weekly result and the real dinner Stake. | "Winner picks dinner. Next week, rematch. Who would you challenge?" |

Do not replace a real result with a fabricated win or imply an unapproved animation is already shipped. For development content, show the design process and label mockups as concepts.

[TikTok's official Creative Codes](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/creative-codes) recommends vertical 9:16 footage, room for the platform UI, and a hook/body/close structure with an emotional or surprising opening. That guidance concerns advertising creative. It supports the proposed format, not a prediction of organic reach.

## A repeatable series and a useful test

Try a weekly pair diary: what each person committed to, an earned look reveal, and the result plus the next agreed Stake. Other proposed hooks are "My partner's character got an upgrade. I want a rematch." and "The outfit I earned by finally keeping this habit." Use the pair's actual experience instead of treating these lines as testimonials.

Compare a character-led clip with a clip showing the same pair, habits, and Stake through the scorecard. Keep the audience, duration, invitation CTA, and destination comparable. Each destination must accurately describe what is available at the time.

Use tagged links and count each new pair once when both finish onboarding and both record a first Check-in within seven days. Record invites and acceptance separately. Watch time, shares, and views help explain the creative's performance; activated pairs and week-two participation test whether it brings the right people. Small organic tests are directional because distribution and viewers vary between posts. For pre-release concept clips, measure interested pairs joining the waitlist rather than claiming product activation.

Implementing unlocks, exporting character clips, producing artwork, launching campaigns, and adding attribution are follow-up work. This proposal adds a marketing use case to the existing character plan without changing its implementation readiness.
